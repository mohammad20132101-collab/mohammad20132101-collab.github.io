How to play
• Click the baseline to slide your striker.
• Drag back from the striker to aim, release to shoot.
• Pocket your color to keep your turn. The red queen must be covered by another of your pieces on the next shot.
• Pocketing the striker is a foul. Pocket all 9 of your color to win.
